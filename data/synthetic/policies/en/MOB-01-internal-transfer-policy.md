# Internal Transfer Policy

## Approval Chain

An internal transfer needs three approvals in order: receiving team sign-off (target day 15), release from the current manager (target day 20), and HR Mobility sign-off. The transfer request itself is approved by HR Mobility at day 10. Each step is tracked against its service-level target and stalls are flagged.

## Release and Handover

The current manager owns the handover plan; the notice period follows the contract grade. A move to a new team is treated as a second onboarding: the new joiner checklist, access re-provisioning and a first 1:1 with the new manager (target day 30) all apply. Approval steps overdue beyond two days are escalated with a summary to the named owner.
