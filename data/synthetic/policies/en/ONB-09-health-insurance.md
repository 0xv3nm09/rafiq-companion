# Health Insurance

## Enrolment

Health insurance is enrolled by HR Benefits with a target of day 14 and takes effect from the first day of the following month at the latest. The employee selects a plan category during pre-boarding. Enrolment status is visible in the journey timeline.

## Dependents

Spouse and children can be added to health insurance after the employee's enrolment is complete; documents required are marriage and birth certificates. Dependent cover is at shared cost per the benefits schedule. Claims questions are answered from this policy; disputes go to HR Benefits.
