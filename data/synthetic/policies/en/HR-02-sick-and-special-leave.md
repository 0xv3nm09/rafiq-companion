# Sick and Special Leave

## Sick Leave

Sick leave is 90 calendar days per year: full pay for the first 45, half pay for the next 30, unpaid thereafter, with a medical certificate from day one. Certificates are uploaded to the portal. Extended illness cases are handled by HR Benefits.

## Hajj and Compassionate Leave

Hajj leave is up to 30 days once per service for eligible employees. Compassionate leave is five days for the loss of an immediate family member. Both are requested through the line manager; the companion can explain eligibility from this policy.
