# Relocation on Transfer

## Allowance Recalculation

Moving to a new emirate or country triggers recalculation of housing and location allowances per the destination band. Payroll applies the change from the effective date of the move. The employee sees the recalculation in the transfer letter.

## Housing and Logistics

Facilities confirms the new location is ready with a target of day 25, including desk, access and parking. Visa transfer for cross-border moves is handled by Visa and Immigration using the ONB-03 process. Shipping of personal effects is per the relocation allowance schedule.
