# Probation and Reviews

## Probation Period

New joiners complete a six-month probation. Structured check-ins with the line manager happen at days 30, 60 and 90, each with written feedback. A probation review without a scheduled 30-day check-in is flagged to the line manager.

## Reviews

The 30/60/90 reviews set expectations and record progress against role benchmarks. Outcomes are advisory to the confirmation decision, which rests with the line manager and HR. Questions about review content should be raised with the line manager first.
