# Access and Account Requests

## Requesting Access

System access is requested through the service desk and approved by the system owner within two working days. New joiners' core access follows the ONB-07 provisioning plan. Access for movers is re-provisioned with a target of day 22 in the mobility journey.

## Lost or Broken Equipment

Lost or damaged equipment is reported to the IT Service Desk the same day; replacements are issued within three working days after a short review. The employee acknowledges the incident in writing. Insurance questions go to HR Benefits.
