# Visa and Relocation

## Entry Permit

For relocating hires the entry permit is sponsored by the employer and normally takes up to 10 working days from document submission. The Visa and Immigration team owns this step and updates the journey once issued. Roughly half of relocating employees call the visa stage the hardest part of the move, so each step is tracked with a named owner.

## Medical Fitness and Emirates ID

After entry, the medical fitness test is booked by the Visa and Immigration team with a target of day 15, and the Emirates ID application follows with a target of day 20. The employee attends in person; the employer arranges appointments. Status of both steps is visible in the journey timeline.

## Residence Visa Stamping

Residence visa stamping is the final immigration step with a target of day 25, after which the passport is returned. Delays beyond target are escalated to the named owner. Family or dependent sponsorship can start once the residence visa is stamped.
