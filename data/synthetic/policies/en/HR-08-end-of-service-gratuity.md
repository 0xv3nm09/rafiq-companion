# End-of-Service Gratuity

## Calculation

Gratuity is 21 days of basic salary per year for the first five years and 30 days per year thereafter, capped at two years' total salary. Unpaid leave days are excluded from service. This explainer is informational; the binding calculation is issued by Payroll.

## Payment

Gratuity is paid within 14 days of the last working day. Deductions for outstanding loans follow the contractual schedule. Disputes go to Payroll, then to HR.
