# Staff Travel and Flight Benefits

## Eligibility

Confirmed employees receive annual concessional travel tickets for themselves and eligible family per the benefits schedule. Eligibility starts after probation confirmation. Standby upgrade rules apply per the travel portal.

## Concessional Fares

Concessional fares are booked through the staff travel portal with applicable taxes only. Ticket counts and zones follow the grade band. The portal shows live availability; this policy explains entitlement.
