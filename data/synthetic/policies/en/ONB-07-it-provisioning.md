# IT Provisioning

## Laptop and Equipment SLA

A laptop is issued by day 3 of onboarding by the IT Service Desk. If a laptop has not been issued by its target day, the employee can ask the companion to chase IT, which raises a priority ticket with the IT Service Desk as owner. Equipment overdue by more than two days is escalated automatically.

## Accounts and Access

IT accounts (email, VPN, core systems) are created by day 2, before the laptop is issued. Access to additional systems is requested through the service desk and granted by the system owner. First-week induction training is booked by Learning and Development with a target of day 7.
