# Role Change Terms

## Grade and Compensation

A role change may adjust grade and salary; the new terms are confirmed in the transfer letter issued by HR Mobility. Salary is aligned to the destination grade band. Questions about the calculation are answered from this policy; contested cases go to HR Mobility.

## Leave Accrual

Annual leave accrual carries over across an internal move; the balance is not reset. Probation does not restart for a lateral move but may restart for a promotion into a different job family. The employee can view their leave balance in the HR self-service portal.
