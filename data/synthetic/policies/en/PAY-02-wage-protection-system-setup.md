# Wage Protection System Setup

## WPS Setup

Salaries are paid through the Wage Protection System to a UAE bank account in the employee's name. Bank and WPS setup has a target of day 10 with Payroll as owner. The employee provides IBAN details during pre-boarding.

## First Salary

The first salary reflects the partial month and includes the housing and transport allowances. Payslips are available in the portal from payday plus one day. Deduction questions are answered from this policy; discrepancies go to Payroll.
