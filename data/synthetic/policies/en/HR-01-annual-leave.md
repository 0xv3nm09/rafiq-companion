# Annual Leave

## Entitlement

Employees accrue 30 calendar days of annual leave per year, prorated for partial years. Accrual continues across internal transfers. Balances are visible in the self-service portal.

## Booking and Approval

Leave is requested in the portal and approved by the line manager within five working days. Requests more than 15 working days require four weeks' notice. Unapproved requests past five working days can be chased through the companion.
