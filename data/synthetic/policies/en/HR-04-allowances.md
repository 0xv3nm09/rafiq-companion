# Allowances

## Housing and Transport

Housing allowance is 30% of basic salary and transport allowance is 10%, per the grade band. Allowances are reviewed at grade changes. The WPS salary setup reflects the full breakdown.

## Education

Education allowance supports up to three children per the grade-dependent schedule, paid termly against receipts. Claims are submitted to Payroll. Eligibility questions are answered from this policy.
