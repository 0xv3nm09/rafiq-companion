# Pre-boarding and Day One

## Before Day One

New joiners receive a pre-boarding pack at offer acceptance covering documents to bring, first-week agenda, and their buddy assignment. HR Onboarding confirms building access and badge readiness before day one. Any document gap should be raised with HR Onboarding before the start date.

## Day One

On day one the line manager welcomes the joiner and walks them through the org chart, desk and facilities. A badge with building access is issued by Facilities on day one. If a badge is not ready, the joiner should ask the companion to chase Facilities.
