# Pension Registration for Emirati Hires

## 30-Day Registration

Emirati hires in the private sector must be registered with GPSSA for pension within 30 days of the start date. HR Onboarding raises the registration; the employee verifies their record in Nafis. This deadline-driven step appears as a first-class task on the joiner's own timeline.

## Nafis Benefits

Nafis benefits for eligible Emirati employees include a salary top-up, child allowance and reduced employee pension contributions. Enrolment is guided by HR Benefits. Aggregated, non-individual localization progress is reported to the entity.
