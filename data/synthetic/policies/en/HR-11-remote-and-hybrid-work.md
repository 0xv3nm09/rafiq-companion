# Remote and Hybrid Work

## Eligibility

Roles are tagged on-site, hybrid or remote by the function. Hybrid roles default to two remote days per week. Frontline and operational roles remain on-site.

## Approval

Permanent remote arrangements require line manager and function-head approval with a six-month review. Ad-hoc remote days are requested in the portal. Equipment for home offices follows IT-01.
